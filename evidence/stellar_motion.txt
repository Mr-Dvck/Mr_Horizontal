# Stellar Motion Observations

This file contains observations of stellar motion, confirming stars rotate
around fixed points.

## Observation #1: Northern Hemisphere

**Location:** Northern Hemisphere
**Fixed point:** Polaris (North Star)
**Motion:** Stars rotate counterclockwise around Polaris
**Conclusion:** Celestial sphere rotates around fixed point at North Pole

## Observation #2: Southern Hemisphere

**Location:** Southern Hemisphere
**Fixed point:** Southern Cross region
**Motion:** Stars rotate clockwise around Southern Cross
**Conclusion:** Celestial sphere rotates around fixed point at South Pole

## Observation #3: Equator

**Location:** Equator
**Fixed points:** Polaris on northern horizon, Southern Cross on southern horizon
**Motion:** Stars rise in east, set in west, rotate around both poles
**Conclusion:** Celestial sphere rotates around both poles

## Observation #4: Constellation Visibility

**Observation:** Stars from opposite hemisphere never visible
- Northern Hemisphere: Never see Southern Cross
- Southern Hemisphere: Never see Polaris

**Conclusion:** Stars are relatively close, rotating around fixed points at the
poles of the flat Earth plane.

## Observation #5: Star Trails

**Method:** Long-exposure photography
**Observation:** Star trails form perfect circles centered on Polaris and
Southern Cross
**Conclusion:** Stars rotate around fixed points, not a spinning globe

## The Clincher

On a spherical Earth, you should see different stars from different latitudes,
with a gradual transition as you move north or south. You should also be able to
see stars from the opposite hemisphere when you travel far enough.

**Yet no such transition is observed.** In the Northern Hemisphere, you never
see the Southern Cross. In the Southern Hemisphere, you never see the North
Star.

**This is exactly what you would expect on a flat plane with stars rotating
around fixed points at the center of the plane, not what you would expect on a
sphere where you should see a gradual transition of stars as you move.**
