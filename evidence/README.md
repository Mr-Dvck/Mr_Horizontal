# Evidence Files

This directory contains supporting evidence and data files for the flat Earth model.

## Files

### horizon_observations.txt
Direct observations of the horizon at various altitudes and distances.

### ship_disappearance.txt
Documentation of ships disappearing bottom-first over the horizon, with
magnification restoring the entire vessel.

### water_level_measurements.txt
Measurements of water levels across large bodies of water, confirming they are
perfectly level, not curved.

### sun_angular_size.txt
Measurements of the Sun's angular size throughout the day, confirming it remains
constant at 0.5 degrees.

### stellar_motion.txt
Observations of stellar motion, confirming stars rotate around fixed points.

### flight_path_data.txt
Flight path data showing that long-distance flights follow straight lines on a
flat Earth map, not great circles on a sphere.

### satellite_verification.txt
Documentation of attempts to independently verify satellites, all of which have
failed.
